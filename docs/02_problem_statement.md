# 02 Problem Statement

Florence Inc. holds security evidence from many parts of its environment. Security teams need one consistent way to answer:

1. Is a security condition actually present?
2. Is the evidence sufficient to say so?
3. Which control is affected?
4. What risk does it create?
5. How should that risk be treated?
6. What remediation is appropriate?
7. Did the remediation work?
8. What decision should management make?

## Why it matters
Florence handles customer personal data and payment data. A single gap, such as an administrative port open to the internet or a privileged account without MFA, can lead to a breach of data that customers and partners expect to be protected.

## Who is affected
- **Customers** whose personal and payment data Florence holds.
- **Security and IT teams** who must triage and fix findings.
- **Executives** who must decide where to spend time and money.
- **Auditors and partners** who ask for evidence that controls work.

## What SentinelBridge adds
Security findings do not stand alone. Each one is traced to the evidence that produced it, the control it relates to, the risk it creates, the remediation required and the verification performed afterwards.
